About Me

Hi, I'm Kelly Lenge, and I'm majoring in Marketing at the University of Miami. I've always been drawn to creativity, communication, and the ways people and brands connect with others. Marketing allows me to combine those passions while learning how to turn ideas into meaningful stories that inspire people.

Over the years, I've realized that experiences outside the classroom shape who you are just as much as academics do. I danced competitively for 15 years, which taught me discipline, teamwork, and what it means to truly work hard for something you care about. More recently, I studied abroad in Barcelona, where I developed a love for travel and gained a new perspective on the world. Both experiences helped me build confidence, independence, and a strong sense of determination.

I'm passionate about understanding people, finding creative ways to communicate, and using marketing to make an impact. I love that it brings together strategy and imagination, allowing me to approach problems from both an analytical and creative point of view.

Outside of school, I enjoy traveling, staying active, and spending time with friends and family. I value experiences that challenge me, teach me something new, and help me grow as a person. As I continue my journey, I hope to build a career where I can bring ideas to life, connect with others, and make a positive difference through creativity and marketing.
