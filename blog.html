Seeing the world

Posted on November 1, 2025

Before studying abroad, I had never been outside the country. Traveling always seemed exciting, but it was not until I lived in Barcelona that I truly understood how much it could change me. The city’s vibrant energy, incredible food, and slower pace taught me to appreciate each moment instead of rushing to the next thing.

During that semester, I visited Morocco, Iceland, Switzerland, Florence, Dublin, Lisbon, Budapest, Madrid, Paris, and London. Each trip brought new lessons and fresh perspectives. I learned to adapt quickly, plan ahead, and stay calm when things didn’t go according to plan.

Traveling on my own also made me more independent. Navigating unfamiliar places and cultures gave me confidence and helped me understand how people around the world live differently while sharing common values of connection, family, and happiness.

Studying abroad taught me more than any classroom could. It helped me discover more about myself and the world, showing me there is always something new to learn and experience. Now, traveling is a passion I plan to carry with me throughout my life.

Dancing through life

Posted on October 25, 2025

I started dancing when I was four years old, and it has been a part of my life ever since. What began as a fun activity quickly became something I truly loved. I went on to dance competitively for 15 years, and over that time it shaped me into the person I am today. Dance taught me discipline, focus, and what it really means to work hard for something you care about.

I had amazing coaches who pushed me to be my best every single day. We spent around 40 hours a week in the studio practicing, sweating, and sometimes even crying, all working toward the same goal. It was challenging, but it taught me that success does not come without effort. You have to show up, stay consistent, and push through the tough moments to achieve something great.

Through dance, I learned how to handle pressure, how to stay confident, and how to lead by example. Every competition and performance taught me to believe in myself, even when things felt uncertain. Dance built my confidence from a young age and helped me grow into a strong and determined woman.

What I love most about dance is how it lets me express myself. It is a way to communicate without words, to turn emotion into movement. Dance gave me confidence, strength, and purpose, and it continues to remind me that with hard work and passion, anything is possible.

Home cooked meals

Posted on August 14, 2025

My love for cooking started pretty recently, but it has quickly become one of my favorite things to do. Growing up, my mom was and still is an amazing cook. Every meal she made brought people together, and I always admired how she made it look so easy while putting so much care into it. From her homemade meatballs and chicken parmesan to her chicken noodle soup, crabcakes, steak, salads, bolongese, and hummus, everything she made had its own special touch. Food was always a big part of our home, and it created memories that I still think about today.

I never cooked much myself until I got to college, but once I started, I realized how much I enjoyed it. When I moved away from home, I missed her meals more than I expected. That inspired me to start learning her recipes and trying them on my own. At first, I called her constantly while cooking, asking what I was doing wrong or how to fix something that did not turn out quite right. Over time, I began to understand the rhythm of it, the timing, the flavor, and the patience it takes to make something great.

Cooking has become something that relaxes me. It gives me time to slow down, focus, and create something from scratch. I love the feeling of finishing a meal and knowing I made it myself. It reminds me of home and all the memories that come with it.

What I love most about cooking is that it is always a learning process. There is always a new recipe to try or a new flavor to explore. It makes me feel creative, independent, and connected to something familiar at the same time.
